# MayDay
Offline Disaster Response System:
> A community-mode, offline-first mobile platform for rural disaster response: location-tagged SOS, mesh-based rescue coordination, a corroboration-driven trust engine, and a shared offline resource map.

**Problem Statement :** Disaster Response Without Infrastructure — develop a mesh-network emergency communication system for disaster-hit rural areas with no internet or power. Must support location-tagged SOS signals, rescue coordination, and a shared resource map offline.

**Solution :** We are building an offline-first disaster response platform for rural areas where internet, cellular networks, and power may fail completely. The app uses Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct mesh networking to let nearby smartphones relay location-tagged SOS alerts, hazard reports, and resource information from phone to phone until they reach a volunteer or NGO responder. A corroboration-based trust engine increases the confidence of alerts when multiple independent devices confirm the same incident, while volunteers can coordinate rescues and share available food, shelter, and medical resources—all without requiring a central server or internet connection.
